## Problem

Shoppers who want to buy greener have no quick way to judge a product's environmental impact while they are on the product page.

## What I built

Built with a team at AGI Ventures Canada Hackathon 3.0. A browser extension reads the product page, gives it an AI EcoScore from 1.0 to 5.0, and suggests more sustainable alternatives on Amazon with prices side by side. A Python backend calls the OpenAI API for the analysis and the search.

## Result

Won Best AI for Environmental Impact at AGI Ventures Canada Hackathon 3.0.
